Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Where is the Guidance Center located?

We are located at the Mabini Building, 3rd Floor, College Guidance and Counselling Center, De La Salle Lipa.

What are your office hours?

Our office hours are:

  • Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 1:30 PM
  • Closed on weekends and holidays

Counseling Services

How do I schedule a counseling session?

You can schedule a session by:

  1. Visiting our office at Mabini Building, 3rd Floor
  2. Setting an appointment online at DLSL Counseling Portal
  3. Emailing collegeguidance.office@dlsl.edu.ph

Walk-in consultations are also accommodated based on counselor availability.

Is counseling confidential?

Yes, all counseling sessions are confidential. Information shared during sessions will not be disclosed to anyone without your written consent, except in situations where there is risk of harm to yourself or others, or when required by law.

Is there a limit to counseling sessions?

No, there is no limit to the number of counseling sessions. Students may schedule sessions as needed throughout their time at the university.

Mental Health Resources

What should I do if I'm worried about a friend?

If you're concerned about a friend:

  1. Express your concern in a caring, non-judgmental way
  2. Listen without trying to "fix" their problems
  3. Encourage them to seek professional help
  4. Offer to accompany them to the Guidance Center
  5. If it's an emergency, contact campus security or the Guidance Office immediately
Are there online mental health resources available?

Yes, we recommend these online resources:

  • Philippine Mental Health Association: www.pmha.org.ph
  • National Center for Mental Health: Crisis Hotline 0917-899-8727 (USAP)
  • MindNation: mindnation.com
