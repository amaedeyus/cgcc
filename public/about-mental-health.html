What is Mental Health?

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act as we cope with life. Good mental health isn't just the absence of mental health problems. It also means having positive characteristics like resilience, self-esteem, and the ability to manage emotions.

Common Mental Health Concerns

Students may experience:

  • Anxiety: Excessive worry that's hard to control
  • Depression: Persistent sadness and loss of interest
  • Stress: Overwhelming pressure from academics and life
  • Sleep problems: Difficulty sleeping or sleeping too much
  • Relationship issues: Challenges with friends, family, or partners

Self-Help Strategies
  • Practice regular physical activity
  • Maintain a balanced diet and sleep schedule
  • Try relaxation techniques (deep breathing, meditation)
  • Stay connected with supportive people
